Privacy Policy

Talefare (beta) · Last updated: 21 November 2026

Talefare is a personal app for tracking the books, movies, shows and audio you want to consume, are consuming, or have finished. It is run by an individual developer. This page explains what information the app uses, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. Questions go to hello@talefare.com.

What we collect

  • Account details (only if you create an account): your email address and a password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are not visible to us in readable form.
  • Your library: the titles you add, their status (for example Up Next, In Progress, Completed), ratings, notes, tags, dates, custom lists, progress, and your app settings.
  • Notification details (only if you allow notifications): a device push token and your notification preferences, so we can send the alerts you ask for.
  • Photos you choose: if you use screenshot import, only the images you pick are used. The app does not use your camera or microphone.

We do not use advertising or analytics tracking tools in the app, we do not sell your information, and we do not build advertising profiles.

Guest mode

You can use Talefare as a guest. In guest mode your library is stored only on your device and is not uploaded to our servers. Some features (such as syncing and AI features) need an account.

Where your data is stored

Your library is stored on your device. If you create an account, it is also stored on our backend, hosted by Supabase, so it can sync between your devices and be restored if you change phones.

AI features

Talefare has optional AI features, such as short summaries, reasons for recommendations, mood-based suggestions, a taste summary, and reading titles out of screenshots you import. You can turn them off in the app's settings.

  • When an AI feature runs, the app sends the information needed for that feature to our backend, which passes it to an AI provider: Anthropic (primary) and OpenAI (used as a backup if the first request fails). This can include details about titles in your library (such as titles, creators, genres, your ratings and status) and, for screenshot import, the images you selected.
  • We do not send your email address or password to these providers.
  • AI-written text can be wrong or incomplete. Treat it as a suggestion.

Other services the app contacts

To find titles, covers, availability and previews, the app and our backend contact these services. They receive the search text or title identifiers needed for the request, and they have their own privacy policies:

  • TMDB and TheTVDB (movies and shows), Open Library and Google Books (books), Apple iTunes Search and Spotify (audio),
  • Watchmode (where a title can be streamed, rented or bought; looked up by our backend),
  • Expo, Apple and Google (delivering push notifications).

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your account data until you delete your account. You can delete your account in the app (Profile → Data & Privacy → Delete Account), which removes your account and the library data stored on our backend. Data that exists only on your device is removed when you delete the app. Backups held by our hosting provider may take a short time to expire.

Your choices

  • Use the app as a guest, so nothing is uploaded.
  • Turn AI features off in settings.
  • Turn notifications off in the app or in your phone's settings.
  • Delete your account, or email us to ask what we hold or to have it corrected or removed.

Children

Talefare is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, and Talefare is beta software, so please do not rely on it as your only copy of something irreplaceable.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest version, and we will make clear in the app if a change is significant.
