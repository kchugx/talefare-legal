Made for people who enjoy more than one kind of story

One library instead of a different app for every format.

One library

Search for a movie, show, book, podcast or audiobook, add it, and give it a status: up next, in progress, completed or dropped.

Pick up where you left off

Track progress the way each format works: pages for books, episodes for shows, minutes for audio. Rate and take notes as you go.

Know where to watch

See which services stream a title, or where you can rent or buy it, without opening five apps.

Never miss a new episode

Get a notification when a show you follow has a new episode, or when a title lands on a streaming service. You choose which alerts you get.

Find what to enjoy next

Recommendations based on your own library, each with a short reason. Not sure what to pick tonight? Tell it your mood and it narrows the list.

See your taste

Look back at what you finished, your favorite genres and creators, and a year in review.

Bring your lists with you

Import from a spreadsheet, paste a list, or pick screenshots and let Talefare find the titles in them.

Phone and browser

Open Talefare in Safari, Chrome or Edge on your iPhone, iPad or computer. Your library lives on your device first, so it is quick, and signing in keeps it synced everywhere you use it.

Private by design

  • Your data stays yours. We don't sell your information, and the privacy policy spells out exactly what the app uses.
  • AI is optional. Summaries and recommendation reasons can be spoiler-aware, and you can turn every AI feature off in settings.
  • Delete everything in the app. Profile, then Data & Privacy, then Delete Account removes your account and your synced library.
